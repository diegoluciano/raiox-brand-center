01 · Identity

A ring, a building, one flag.

The mark is a yellow ring that opens at the bottom like a magnifier, with a city hall and a flag inside. It lives next to a bold “RAIO-X CÂMARA” wordmark: white on dark, purple on light.

What the audit says. White on Yellow is 1.5:1 and white on Cyan is 1.8:1. Labels on yellow and cyan use Ink (10.4:1 and 8.6:1). Yellow works for icons and small type on Ink (10.4:1).
